This app does one thing: it gently reminds a Muslim to send Salawat — a short
blessing on the Prophet Muhammad — throughout their ordinary day. 332 Arabic
phrases are bundled in the binary for the reminders. No account, no internet,
no data collected. Just a quiet reminder.

The app opens in Arabic — tap the globe button (EN) in the top corner to switch
to English. The settings have two tabs: "Visual Reminder" and "Voice Reminder".

HOW TO SEE IT
1) The widget: long-press the home screen > "+" > search "Salah Reminder" >
   Add Widget. A new phrase appears every hour.
2) Hourly reminders (Visual Reminder tab): toggle "Hourly Reminder" and allow
   the notification prompt. The first one fires at the next whole hour, so
   nothing appears immediately.
3) Voice reminder (Voice Reminder tab, NEW in this version, off by default):
   toggle it on and allow notifications. Tap "Preview sound" to hear the
   2-second recorded phrase bundled with the app. Scheduled voice reminders are
   standard local notifications that use this clip as their notification
   sound, inside the time periods and days the user picks. There is no
   background audio mode; the ringer switch and ringer volume apply as for any
   notification.

Please test in Airplane Mode: the app makes no network requests at all. Nothing
is loaded from a URL, there is no server, no analytics and no ads. iPad is
supported.
