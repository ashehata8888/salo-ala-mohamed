This app does one thing: it gently reminds a Muslim to send Salawat — a short
blessing on the Prophet Muhammad — throughout their ordinary day. 332 Arabic
phrases are bundled in the binary so a different one appears each hour. No
account, no internet, no data collected. Just a quiet reminder.

HOW TO SEE IT
1) The widget (main feature): long-press the home screen > "+" > search
   "Salah Reminder" > Add Widget. A new phrase appears every hour.
2) Hourly reminders: toggle the second row on the main screen and allow the
   notification prompt. The first one fires at the next whole hour, so nothing
   appears immediately.

The app opens in Arabic — tap "English" in the Language row to switch.

Please test in Airplane Mode: the app makes no network requests at all. Nothing
is loaded from a URL, there is no server, no analytics and no ads. iPad is
supported.
