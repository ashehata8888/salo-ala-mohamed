A quiet, simple app that reminds you to send Salawat on the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ every time you unlock your phone.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever sends one Salah upon me, Allah sends ten upon him."

Features:
• A gentle on-screen reminder each time you unlock your device.
• An optional hourly reminder you can switch on or off at any time.
• Over 330 authentic Salawat phrases in Arabic, rotating automatically so the words stay fresh.
• Control how long each reminder stays on screen.
• Pause reminders temporarily whenever you need to.
• Arabic-first interface with complete English support.
• Works entirely offline.

Privacy:
This app collects nothing about you. No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

A note on permissions:
The app requests the "Display over other apps" permission to show the reminder on unlock, and runs a background service solely for that purpose.
